Booking terms & conditions.

These terms apply to all Masterclass Tickets and Partner Packages for The Ultimate Masterclass Dubai.

Organiser: The Ultimate Masterclass
Booking enquiries, cancellations and ticket transfers: info@theultimatemasterclass.co.uk

1. What your booking includes

  • Masterclass Ticket includes access to the business masterclass and the experiences listed at checkout and in your booking confirmation.
  • Partner Package includes the welcome brunch, Arabian night, desert experience and yacht experience. The business masterclass is exclusive to Masterclass Ticket holders.

Flights, accommodation and travel insurance are not included in either ticket type. You are responsible for arranging and paying for these separately. Any transfers included in your ticket will be listed in your booking confirmation. If we share hotel recommendations or rates, you book directly with the accommodation provider.

2. Payment and booking confirmation

Your place is confirmed when we send your booking confirmation following full payment or receipt of the first payment under an agreed instalment plan. Your total price and instalment due dates will be shown at checkout or in your booking confirmation.

If you miss an instalment, we will contact you and give you a reasonable opportunity to bring your payments up to date. If the payment remains outstanding, we may cancel your booking, subject to your statutory rights.

3. Guest cancellations and non-attendance

If the event goes ahead and you cancel or are unable to attend, your ticket payments are non-refundable except where a refund is required by law. This applies to both ticket types, whether you paid in full or by instalments.

This includes non-attendance due to illness, personal circumstances, flight cancellations or delays, missed connections, passport or visa issues, severe weather, natural disasters, war or armed conflict, airspace closures or changes to official travel advice.

You are responsible for any cancellation, rebooking or additional costs for flights, accommodation and other arrangements you booked independently, subject to any rights you have against us, your travel provider or your insurer. Please email us as soon as possible if you are unable to attend.

4. Reselling your ticket

You may resell your ticket privately. You are responsible for finding a buyer and arranging payment directly with them.

Once you have a buyer, email us with your booking reference and the buyer’s full name and email address so we can update the booking and send them the event details. If you are paying by instalments, please contact us before completing the sale so we can confirm how any outstanding payments will be handled.

The transfer is complete once we confirm it by email. The new ticket holder will be subject to these booking terms.

5. Travel documents and insurance

You are responsible for checking passport validity, visa and UAE entry requirements, and official travel advice before booking and again before travelling.

Please arrange suitable travel insurance as soon as you book. Check that it covers travel to the UAE, emergency medical treatment and repatriation, and any activities you plan to take part in, such as the yacht trip, horse riding or quad biking where applicable. Ask your insurer what cover is available for your event ticket and independently booked travel if you have to cancel or experience a delay or missed departure. Check the policy exclusions for war, natural disasters, airspace closures and changes to official travel advice.

6. Programme and activity changes

We may make reasonable changes to timings, venues, hosts or individual activities for safety or operational reasons. If an activity cannot go ahead, we may offer a comparable alternative where available. If a change significantly affects your booking, we will contact you as soon as reasonably possible and explain your options.

7. If we cancel, postpone or significantly change the event

Circumstances outside our reasonable control may require us to change, postpone or cancel the event. These may include severe weather, floods or other natural disasters, war or armed conflict, civil unrest, terrorism, government restrictions, airport or airspace closures, widespread flight cancellations or a public health emergency.

If we cancel the event before it starts, we will refund the ticket payments you have made for the cancelled booking. If we postpone the event or make a significant change before it starts, you can accept the revised booking or cancel for a refund of your ticket payments. If the event is cut short after it starts, we will explain any refund or price reduction due for the services affected.

These ticket refunds cover payments made to us for the event. Any reimbursement of costs you incurred for independently booked travel or accommodation will depend on our legal responsibility.

8. Safety and participation

Attendees must follow the safety instructions given by the organiser, venues and activity providers. Some activities may have age, health or other participation requirements. Please contact us before booking if you have questions about taking part and let us know in advance about any accessibility needs or allergies relevant to your booking.

9. Statutory rights

These terms do not affect your statutory rights. Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or any other liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.
