Good to Know

Questions.

When is the Dubai edition?

14th – 21st April 2027, in Dubai. The five programme days (brunch, desert, wellness, masterclass and yacht) take place within those dates, and the rest of your stay is yours to relax and explore.

Who is it for?

Ambitious business owners and entrepreneurs across all industries, including hair, beauty, aesthetics and SPMU. If you want fresh ideas, a clearer direction and connections with people who understand the journey, it's for you.

Are flights and hotels included?

No. Guests arrange their own travel and accommodation.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Pay £1,800 in full, or 5 monthly payments of £399.40 (£1,997 in total).

Can I bring my partner?

Yes. Partners need their own Partner ticket (£500), purchased separately. It covers the welcome brunch, the Arabian night & desert adventure and the yacht party. Partners don't attend the masterclass or wellness sessions.

Are tickets refundable?

Bookings are non-refundable. Message us on WhatsApp if your plans change and we'll do what we can.
