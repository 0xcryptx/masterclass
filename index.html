Good to Know

Questions.

When is the Dubai edition?

April 2027. The exact dates will be announced on our Instagram and to everyone holding a ticket.

Who is it for?

Anyone in the beauty industry: practitioners, injectors, skin specialists, beauty therapists, salon and clinic owners. If you want advanced education and a serious network, it's for you.

Are flights and hotels included?

No. Guests arrange their own travel and accommodation. Transport between the Marriott and the beach club is included on Day 1.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Pay £1,800 in full, or 5 monthly payments of £399.40 (£1,997 in total).

Can I bring my partner?

Yes. The £500 Partner ticket covers the pool party, the yacht and the Arabian night. Partners don't attend the education or wellness sessions.

Are tickets refundable?

Bookings are non-refundable. Message us on WhatsApp if your plans change and we'll do what we can.
