Good to Know

Questions.

When is the Dubai edition?

April 2027. The exact dates will be announced on our Instagram and to everyone holding a ticket.

Who is it for?

Aesthetic practitioners, injectors, skin specialists and clinic owners who want advanced education and a serious network.

Are flights and hotels included?

No. Guests arrange their own travel and accommodation. Transport between the Marriott and the beach club is included on Day 1.

Can I only attend the masterclass?

Yes. The Day 3 ticket covers the full doctor-led masterclass with lunch and refreshments.

Can I bring my partner?

Yes. The Partners Package covers the beach club on Day 1 and the yacht on Day 4. Partners don't attend the education or wellness days.

Are tickets refundable?

Bookings are non-refundable. Message us on WhatsApp if your plans change and we'll do what we can.
